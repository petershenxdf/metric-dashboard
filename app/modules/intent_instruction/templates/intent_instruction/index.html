Intent Instruction

Converts chat messages into a structured instruction state through a two-stage pipeline: a router decides whether a message is actionable, ambiguous, a meta-query, or off-topic; an extractor then turns actionable messages into an InstructionDelta that the service layer applies to the running StructuredInstruction.
